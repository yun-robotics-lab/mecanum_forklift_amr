## Cause (verified by tracing /dock_pose, /filtered_dock_pose and the robot track)
The server computes the correct target every time (e.g. pallet +10 deg: target (0.537, -0.064, 9.9 deg), correct). The
stock approach controller outputs vx and wz only. With a lateral/yaw offset it reaches the target line late, passes the target
without ever being inside `docking_threshold` (1 cm), keeps driving to ~0.78 m, then spins at 0.3 rad/s until the
workflow timeout. For small offsets it stops with 5-6 mm / 3.4-3.6 deg residual error and still reports success
(threshold is position-only).
