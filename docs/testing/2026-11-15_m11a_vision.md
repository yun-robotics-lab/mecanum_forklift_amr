## Result against the criterion (<= 5 mm, <= 1 deg at 0.5-1.5 m): **partly met**
- Met for position at <= 0.89 m (<= 3.6 mm in all 9 cases); lateral and height errors <= 1.5 mm everywhere.
- Not met: 1.19 m fork 0 (7.3 mm, 9 deg), no detection at 1.49 m (marker ~30 px wide), and rotation > 1 deg in 3 of 9
  cases at <= 0.89 m (max 1.7 deg; near-frontal square-marker pose ambiguity, **cause not verified**).
- Cause of the depth bias (verified on one frame): the rendered marker is ~1 px narrower than the geometry
  (78 px measured vs 79.1 px expected at 0.59 m), because texture edges blur. Growing each corner outward by 0.75 px
  removes it (0.59 m: error 8.0 -> 0.0 mm). This is a **sim-only calibration** (`corner_expand_px`, set 0.0 on hardware).
- Camera 1280x960 was tried and rejected: the sim camera then ran at ~0.7 image/s (vs 6-12/s at 640x480).

## Notes / lessons
- Assigning `bytes` to `Image.data` in rclpy takes ~130 ms; `array.array('B', ...)` 5 us. The debug image
  starved the TF listener until this was fixed. `cv_bridge` crashes here (system numpy/OpenCV vs user-site 2.x/5.0), so it is not used.
- The callback never blocks on TF; detections are queued and transformed when TF reaches their stamp.
- Sim RTF was ~0.4-0.5 (image ~6 Hz wall).
